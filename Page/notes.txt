Drei Bünde Map:
I mostly based this map off of Sidonius's map of 1367 noble positions in Graubünden. Note that this is actually outdated, since I want to simulate the formation of the leagues.
By that time, the Lords of Belfort and perhaps other families had already gone extinct. In fact it was the extinction of the noble houses that largely spurred the establishment of league alliances.
I will disagree with Sidonius on one point though. My research indicates the existence of a Lordship of Castrisch separate from that of Belmont, though they were held by the same individuals.
I eventually didn't end up making this change. It's a sort of grey area but it would be impossible for me to verify the status of every lordship as separate from others or not.
So I think I will end up just using the original map for the sure boundaries and also having transparent boundaries drawn beneath them for the later separate jurisdictions.

So far I had the idea for two variants. The first includes only the lands that the Three Leagues had at their maximal extent, including Untercalven/Vinschgau and Misox.
The second is the expanded one that also includes all vassals and subjects later obtained. Even though control of the Tre Pievi (three parishes) was extremely temporal we're keeping it around.
The final major thing to note is that I'm ignoring all lands that were added upon the formation of the canton and had not been previously controlled, those being Tarasp and Haldenstein.
Also worth noting is that there are no official propotions for the League of the Ten Jurisdictions' flag and coats of arms, although there are commonly used standards, so I'm not technically wrong with my flag.
While the Grey League had two different coats of arms for quite a while, in 1505 they were standardized. I picked the half-and-half one because while it does look less unique, it's more different from the other leagues.
All three coats of arms (the League of the House of God has had one consistently) had shield holders, those being the "Wild Man", St. George, and Mary, respectively, though all three have since been removed.
Also note that my in-game narrative for the leagues' formations are not entirely accurate. Nobles often joined or worked with the leagues rather than rebelling, as did the Abbot of Disentis.

To finalize, here's a timeline of Graubünden's territorial evolution.
    1337 - Extinction of the Barons of Vaz
    1348 - Chiavenna, Bormio, and Poschiavo ceded by the lords of Matsch to the dukes of Milan
    1355 - The Counts of Toggenburg obtain control of their lands in Graubünden, including Maienfeld, Davos, Belfort, and Churwalden
1367 - League of God's House formed
    1371 - Extinction of the Lords of Belmont
    1383 - Safien acquired by the Lords of Rhäzüns
1394 - Bergün joins the League of God's House
1395 - Grey League formed
1399 - Hohentrins joins the Grey League
1400 - Rheinwald joins the Grey League
1406 - Schams joins the Grey League
1408 - Poschiavo joins the League of God's House
1415 - Müstair and Untercalven join the League of God's House
1428 - The Free Men of Laax purchase their freedom from the Counts of Werdenberg-Sargans
1434 - The Free Men of Laax join the Grey League
1436 - League of the Ten Jurisdictions formed
1440 - Heinzenberg and Thusis join the Grey League
1441 - Cazis Abbey (a women's abbey with very few lands) joins the Grey League
1452 - Ortenstein joins the League of God's House
1456 - Obervaz joins the League of God's House
    1464 - Tarasp sold from the Matsch family to the Austrians
1480 - Mesocco and Soazza near Misox join the Grey League, Misox is given to Milan's Gian Giacomo Trivulzio
1496 - Trivulzio with Misox and Calanca joins the Grey League
1509 - Lords of Maienfeld removed
1512 - Bormio, Chiavenna, Valtellina, and the Tre Pievi occupied
1524 - Tre Pievi lost to Gian Giacomo Medici, Three Leagues formally established as a joint state
1618 - Untercalven lost to the Austrians
1620 - Bormio, Chiavenna, and Valtellina occupied by the Austrians during the Thirty Years' War
1613 - Belfort partitioned into Innerbelfort and Ausserbelfort
1622 - Most of the east (Unterengadin, Schiers, Castels, Klosters, Müstair) temporarily occupied by the Austrians, Castels partitioned into Luzein and Jenaz
1631 - After constant rebellion, Klosters is finally freed from occupation
1639 - Austrian-occupied subjects retaken (but not Untercalven)
1649 - Müstair regained by treaty with Austria, Schiers, Castels, and Klosters purchase their independence from Austria
1652 - Unterengadin regained by treaty with Austria
1679 - Schiers partitioned into Schiers and Seewis
1695 - Rhäzüns directly controlled by the Habsburgs through feudal means
1788 - Internal split of Ortenstein into Ortenstein im Boden and Ortenstein im Berg
1797 - Bormio, Chiavenna, and Valtellina become part of the Cisalpine Republic
1798 - The Three Leagues come under the control of the Helvetic Republic
1799 - The Three Leagues are merged into the canton of Raetia under the Helvetic Republic
1803 - Act of Mediation, Haldenstein and Tarasp mediatized from Austria (Haldenstein joins the four villages to become the fifth)
1809 - Rhäzüns transferred to Napoleonic France
1815 - The return of Rhäzüns to the canton's control is planned at the Congress of Vienna
1819 - Rhäzüns becomes the last area to join the modern canton
1863 - Cavaione is transferred to Switzerland from Italy in a minor border adjustment
